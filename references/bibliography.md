# 参考文献

按指南涉及的方法列出原始来源；具体结论见对应案例。年份按正式卷期著录。

- **MiFish-U/E**. Miya et al. (2015). MiFish, a set of universal PCR primers for metabarcoding environmental DNA from fishes: detection of more than 230 subtropical marine species. *Royal Society Open Science*. [DOI: 10.1098/rsos.150088](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.150088).
- **teleo / Tele01**. Valentini et al. (2016). Next‐generation monitoring of aquatic biodiversity using environmental DNA metabarcoding. *Molecular Ecology*. [DOI: 10.1111/mec.13428](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.13428).
- **Tele02 相关专著**. Taberlet et al. (2018). Environmental DNA: For Biodiversity Research and Monitoring. *Oxford University Press*. [DOI: 10.1093/oso/9780198767220.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198767220.001.0001).
- **MiDeca**. Komai et al. (2019). Development of a new set of PCR primers for eDNA metabarcoding decapod crustaceans. *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.3.33835](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.3.33835).
- **Scle-12S**. Shinzato et al. (2021). Novel Mitochondrial DNA Markers for Scleractinian Corals and Generic-Level Environmental DNA Metabarcoding. *Frontiers in Marine Science*. [DOI: 10.3389/fmars.2021.758207](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2021.758207).
- **BirT**. Thalinger et al. (2026). BirT: A Novel Primer Pair for Avian Environmental DNA Metabarcoding. *Environmental DNA*. [DOI: 10.1002/edn3.70255](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.70255).
- **MiMammal**. Ushio et al. (2017). Environmental DNA enables detection of terrestrial mammals from forest pond water. *Molecular Ecology Resources*. [DOI: 10.1111/1755-0998.12690](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.12690).
- **MiBird-U**. Ushio et al. (2018). Demonstration of the potential of environmental DNA as a tool for the detection of avian species. *Scientific Reports*. [DOI: 10.1038/s41598-018-22817-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-22817-5).
- **Amph16S 等**. Sakata et al. (2022). Development and evaluation of PCR primers for environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding of Amphibia. *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.6.76534](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.6.76534).
- **16SOph1/2**. Okanishi et al. (2023). Development of two new sets of PCR primers for eDNA metabarcoding of brittle stars (Echinodermata, Ophiuroidea). *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.7.94298](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.7.94298).
- **Cep16S_D/O**. Wu et al. (2025). Development of universal PCR primers for the environmental DNA metabarcoding of cephalopod (Mollusca) diversity. *Marine Environmental Research*. [DOI: 10.1016/j.marenvres.2025.107094](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marenvres.2025.107094).
- **μCeta**. Ushio et al. (2025). μCeta: A Set of Cetacean‐Specific Primers for Environmental DNA Metabarcoding With Minimal Amplification of Non‐Target Vertebrates. *Environmental DNA*. [DOI: 10.1002/edn3.70193](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.70193).
- **Fish_ITS2_F/R**. Ito et al. (2026). Collection of ITS2 region reference sequences for freshwater fish in the Japanese archipelago for environmental DNA metabarcoding applications. *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.10.162895](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.10.162895).
- **UnaSINE1**. Hirayama et al. (2025). Significant Improvement of Environmental DNA Assay by Targeting Retrotransposon Sequences Characteristic to Anguilla Eels. *Environmental DNA*. [DOI: 10.1002/edn3.70197](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.70197).
- **ecoPrimers**. Riaz et al. (2011). ecoPrimers: inference of new DNA barcode markers from whole genome sequence analysis. *Nucleic Acids Research*. [DOI: 10.1093/nar/gkr732](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr732).
- **PrimerMiner**. Elbrecht & Leese (2017). PrimerMiner: an r package for development and in silico validation of DNA metabarcoding primers. *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*. [DOI: 10.1111/2041-210X.12687](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12687).
- **COI BF/BR**. Elbrecht & Leese (2017). Validation and Development of COI Metabarcoding Primers for Freshwater Macroinvertebrate Bioassessment. *Frontiers in Environmental Science*. [DOI: 10.3389/fenvs.2017.00011](https://doi.org/10.3389/fenvs.2017.00011).
- **BF/BR 更正稿**. Elbrecht & Leese (2017). Corrigendum: Validation and Development of COI Metabarcoding Primers for Freshwater Macroinvertebrate Bioassessment. *Frontiers in Environmental Science*. [DOI: 10.3389/fenvs.2017.00038](https://doi.org/10.3389/fenvs.2017.00038).
- **MOL16S / SPH16S**. Klymus et al. (2017). Environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding assays to detect invasive invertebrate species in the Great Lakes. *PLOS ONE*. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0177643](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0177643).
- **昆虫多标记设计**. Marquina et al. (2019). New mitochondrial primers for metabarcoding of insects, designed and evaluated using in silico methods. *Molecular Ecology Resources*. [DOI: 10.1111/1755-0998.12942](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.12942).
- **鱼类引物综合比较**. Zhang et al. (2020). A comprehensive and comparative evaluation of primers for metabarcoding eDNA from fish. *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*. [DOI: 10.1111/2041-210X.13485](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.13485).
- **中欧鱼类 mock 比较**. Macher et al. (2023). Evaluating five primer pairs for environmental DNA metabarcoding of Central European fish species based on mock communities. *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.7.103856](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.7.103856).
- **fwhF2 / EPTDr2n**. Leese et al. (2021). Improved freshwater macroinvertebrate detection from environmental DNA through minimized nontarget amplification. *Environmental DNA*. [DOI: 10.1002/edn3.177](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.177).
- **qMiSeq**. Ushio et al. (2018). Quantitative monitoring of multispecies fish environmental DNA using high-throughput sequencing. *Metabarcoding and Metagenomics*. [DOI: 10.3897/mbmg.2.23297](https://doi.org/10.3897/mbmg.2.23297).
- **early-pooling**. Ushio et al. (2022). An efficient early‐pooling protocol for environmental DNA metabarcoding. *Environmental DNA*. [DOI: 10.1002/edn3.337](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.337).
